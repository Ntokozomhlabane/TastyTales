# TastyTales 🍲 
**"Cooking the Stories of Our Ancestors."**

## Overview  
TastyTales is an interactive web app that teaches children about South African culture, heritage, and traditional foods through fun cooking games, storytelling, and cultural exploration.

The app turns learning into play — where every dish tells a story, and every recipe connects children to the roots of their culture.

🧭 Mission
To preserve South African culinary heritage by helping the next generation learn through play, cooking, and storytelling.


## 📱 Features
- 🥣 **Cooking Mini-Games:** Interactive, drag-and-drop gameplay where kids “cook” traditional meals like pap, chakalaka, and vetkoek step-by-step.
- 📖 **Storytime Recipes:** Each dish is paired with an animated story or folktale that shares its cultural background and meaning.
- 🌍 **Cultural Map Of South Africa:** Children can explore each province to learn about its signature dishes, languages, and unique traditions.
- 🌈 **Multilingual Narration:** Voiceovers in English, isiZulu, Afrikaans, Setswana, and Sesotho, teaching both language and culture.
- 🧑🏾‍🏫 **Classroom Mode:** Teachers can use the app for heritage education, tracking learning progress and assigning activities. 
- 🏅 **Gamified Learning:** Earn “Cultural Chef Badges” for completing recipes, learning about provinces, or mastering local ingredients.
